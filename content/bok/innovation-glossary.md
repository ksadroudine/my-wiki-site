## Summary

This glossary defines the terms that appear most often in the Innovation part of the Book of Knowledge. Each entry gives a short plain-language definition and points to the Concept where the term is explained in depth. Terms are listed in alphabetical order.

## Glossary of Innovation Terms

| Term | Meaning | See |
|------|---------|-----|
| Acceleration | The competency of scaling a validated concept into a business unit that can stand on its own. | [Organizational Innovation Capability](#/concept/organizational-innovation-capability) |
| Algorithm, the | A five-step process of question every requirement, delete, simplify, accelerate and automate, documented by Jon McNeill. | [Musk's First-Principles "Algorithm"](#/concept/musk-first-principles-algorithm) |
| Architecture trap | The mistake of applying one organizational structure and incentive system to both early-stage innovation and the mature business. | [Organizational Innovation Capability](#/concept/organizational-innovation-capability) |
| Boundary-straddling | The position between the familiar core of a field and its experimental edge, where creative success is highest. | [Organizational Innovation Capability](#/concept/organizational-innovation-capability) |
| Collaboration trap | The rivalry and distrust that grow between teams for mature products and teams for breakthrough innovation. | [Organizational Innovation Capability](#/concept/organizational-innovation-capability) |
| Corporate ecosystem | Supply chains, platform partnerships and other commercial relationships anchored on an incumbent firm. | [Organizational Innovation Capability](#/concept/organizational-innovation-capability) |
| Discovery | The competency of generating and describing innovation opportunities. | [Organizational Innovation Capability](#/concept/organizational-innovation-capability) |
| First-principles thinking | Breaking a problem down to its most basic elements instead of reasoning by analogy from earlier solutions. | [Musk's First-Principles "Algorithm"](#/concept/musk-first-principles-algorithm) |
| Focused naivete | The pattern in which outsiders, free of a field's norms, connect distant ideas and challenge the status quo. | [Organizational Innovation Capability](#/concept/organizational-innovation-capability) |
| Identity trap | The collapse of who we are into what we do, which makes breakthrough innovation feel like a threat. | [Organizational Innovation Capability](#/concept/organizational-innovation-capability) |
| Incubation | The competency of developing business concepts with evidence. | [Organizational Innovation Capability](#/concept/organizational-innovation-capability) |
| Inflection point | A moment of acute pressure when a system becomes receptive to outsider ideas it had dismissed. | [Organizational Innovation Capability](#/concept/organizational-innovation-capability) |
| Innovation ecosystem | A hub tied to a place, with a research university, early-stage activity and shared specialized resources, oriented to the earliest stages of innovation. | [Organizational Innovation Capability](#/concept/organizational-innovation-capability) |
| Kaizen | The Japanese practice of continuous improvement. | [Apple's Manufacturing Quality Legacy](#/concept/apple-manufacturing-quality-legacy) |
| Outside-in translator | A person employed to bridge an outside field and the organization, such as Natura's cultural translators. | [Organizational Innovation Capability](#/concept/organizational-innovation-capability) |
| Rotator | An academic scientist on loan to an organization for two to four years, as at the National Science Foundation. | [Organizational Innovation Capability](#/concept/organizational-innovation-capability) |
| Single point of failure | A dependency whose loss would stop the entire system, such as the shared chip supply of three businesses. | [Musk's First-Principles "Algorithm"](#/concept/musk-first-principles-algorithm) |
| Smiling curve | A pattern that places low value on manufacturing and high value on design and branding at the two ends of the curve. | [Apple's Manufacturing Quality Legacy](#/concept/apple-manufacturing-quality-legacy) |
| Strategic-innovation function | A permanent, governed organizational function for generating and commercializing new business models repeatedly. | [Organizational Innovation Capability](#/concept/organizational-innovation-capability) |
| Yang-tao-sha | A Chinese term meaning raise, entrap and slaughter, used for building national capability through foreign partners. | [Apple's Manufacturing Quality Legacy](#/concept/apple-manufacturing-quality-legacy) |
