## Summary

This glossary defines the terms that appear most often in the Customer and Go-to-Market part of the Book of Knowledge. Each entry gives a short plain-language definition and points to the Concept where the term is explained in depth. Terms are listed in alphabetical order.

## Glossary of Customer and Go-to-Market Terms

| Term | Meaning | See |
|------|---------|-----|
| Build-measure-learn | A loop in which a minimum viable product is built as an experiment, measured against a hypothesis and used to decide whether to persevere or pivot. | [Lean Startup and Customer Development](#/concept/lean-startup-customer-development) |
| Business model canvas | A description of a business in nine boxes, from value proposition and customer segments to cost structure. | [Lean Startup and Customer Development](#/concept/lean-startup-customer-development) |
| Cohort | A group of customers won in the same period, whose behavior is followed over time. | [Customer Centricity](#/concept/customer-centricity) |
| Customer Development | A method that treats a startup as a search for a repeatable business model, in four steps from customer discovery to company building. | [Lean Startup and Customer Development](#/concept/lean-startup-customer-development) |
| Customer equity | The sum of the projected lifetime values of a company's current and future customers. | [Customer Centricity](#/concept/customer-centricity) |
| Customer lifetime value (CLV) | The projected net profit a firm expects to earn from a customer over the entire relationship. | [Customer Centricity](#/concept/customer-centricity) |
| Customer portfolio management | A way of grouping customers dynamically by the strength of their relationship with the brand, as acquaintances, friends or partners. | [Customer Centricity](#/concept/customer-centricity) |
| Customer-base audit | An analysis of transaction history arranged as a data cube of customers by time. | [Customer Centricity](#/concept/customer-centricity) |
| Data fabric | The layer of a martech stack that collects, manages and assembles internal and external data into unified customer profiles. | [Martech Stack and Go-to-Market Strategy](#/concept/martech-stack-strategy) |
| Earlyvangelist | An early customer who already feels an acute problem, has built a workaround and has or can get a budget. | [Lean Startup and Customer Development](#/concept/lean-startup-customer-development) |
| Feature overshoot | The buildup of unused features that clutters a product and makes the useful features harder to find. | [Behavioral Product Design](#/concept/behavioral-product-design) |
| Five Lenses | Five questions that structure a customer-base audit, from the number of customers to comparison with competitors. | [Customer Centricity](#/concept/customer-centricity) |
| Go-to-market strategy | The plan that aligns sales, marketing and product around the market segments and buyers that the company serves. | [Martech Stack and Go-to-Market Strategy](#/concept/martech-stack-strategy) |
| Growth hacking | Growth treated as a responsibility of the entire organization and driven by rigorous analytics, as an evolution of marketing. | [Lean Startup and Customer Development](#/concept/lean-startup-customer-development) |
| Hook Model | A four-step cycle of trigger, action, variable reward and investment that turns repeated use into habit. | [Behavioral Product Design](#/concept/behavioral-product-design) |
| Identity resolution | Assembling a unified picture of a customer from the different identifiers used across touchpoints. | [Martech Stack and Go-to-Market Strategy](#/concept/martech-stack-strategy) |
| Martech | Marketing technology, meaning the data, applications and services that support marketing strategy, insight and engagement. | [Martech Stack and Go-to-Market Strategy](#/concept/martech-stack-strategy) |
| Minimum viable product | An early version built as an experiment to produce validated learning as fast as possible. | [Lean Startup and Customer Development](#/concept/lean-startup-customer-development) |
| Orchestration | A unified facility for assembling audiences, communications and triggered interactions across touchpoints. | [Martech Stack and Go-to-Market Strategy](#/concept/martech-stack-strategy) |
| Pivot | A substantive change to one or more boxes of the business model canvas, driven by evidence from customers. | [Lean Startup and Customer Development](#/concept/lean-startup-customer-development) |
| Product-centricity | A strategy of building a product and selling it to as many customers as possible, whatever their differing value. | [Customer Centricity](#/concept/customer-centricity) |
| Variable reward | An unpredictable payoff that sustains interest more strongly than a fixed one. | [Behavioral Product Design](#/concept/behavioral-product-design) |
