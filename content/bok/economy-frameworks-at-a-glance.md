## Summary

This reference page lists the named frameworks that appear in the Economy and Geopolitics part of the Book of Knowledge, in one table. Each row says what the framework helps you do and where to read more. They cover the exits from unsustainable public debt, the reasons for the gap between data and perception on affordability, the grouping of countries by digital development and the place-based strategies of Chinese companies.
