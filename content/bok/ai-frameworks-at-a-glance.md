## Summary

This reference page lists the named frameworks that appear in the AI part of the Book of Knowledge, in one table. Each row says what the framework is, what question it helps answer and where to read more. The frameworks are grouped by the stage of AI work they support, from understanding the technology to choosing projects, scaling them, organizing work and managing risk.

## AI Frameworks at a Glance

### Understanding the technology and the decision

| Framework | What it helps you do | See |
|-----------|----------------------|-----|
| CRISP-DM | Run a data mining project through six phases, from business understanding to deployment, with loops back when later findings change earlier assumptions. | [Data Mining Fundamentals](#/concept/data-mining-fundamentals) |
| Prediction, judgment and action | Split any decision into predicting what will happen, judging how much each outcome matters and acting, to see which part a machine can take over. | [Prediction Machines](#/concept/prediction-machines) |
| AI canvas | Answer seven questions about a task, such as the action, the prediction needed and the data available, to find where a prediction machine can be inserted. | [Prediction Machines](#/concept/prediction-machines) |
| Narrow and wide decisions | Decide whether a decision has clear goals, usable data and fast feedback, which suits analytical AI, or contested goals and ambiguity, which suits generative AI as a support. | [Matching AI Type to Decision Type](#/concept/matching-ai-type-to-decision-type) |
| Jagged frontier, Centaur and Cyborg | Recognize that AI ability is uneven across tasks, and choose between dividing work cleanly between person and machine or interleaving effort within one task. | [Working Effectively with Generative AI](#/concept/working-with-generative-ai) |
| Cost of error and type of knowledge | Sort the tasks in a job into four zones, from tasks AI can simply do to tasks where it may only assist, according to how costly a mistake is and whether the knowledge is explicit or tacit. | [AI-Driven Work and Organizational Redesign](#/concept/ai-work-and-organization-redesign) |

| Framework | What it helps you do | See |
|-----------|----------------------|-----|

| Deconstruct, redeploy, reconstruct | Break work into tasks, move each to the best combination of automation, junior staff, senior staff or outside capability, and rebuild the process. | [AI-Driven Work and Organizational Redesign](#/concept/ai-work-and-organization-redesign) |
| Agentic AI mesh | Build an architecture of interchangeable, vendor-neutral parts with built-in governance so that many agents can work together. | [Agentic AI Enterprise Transformation](#/concept/agentic-ai-enterprise-transformation) |

### Governance, safety and risk

| Framework | What it helps you do | See |
|-----------|----------------------|-----|

| Three layers of adaptive governance | Match controls to the system, with rules for narrow systems, outcome testing for opaque ones and ecosystem oversight for connected ones. | [AI Governance and Responsible AI Programs](#/concept/ai-governance-programs) |
| SHARP | Close the accountability, strategy and resource gaps in responsible AI through ownership, built-in ethics, business alignment, rewards and judgment. | [AI Governance and Responsible AI Programs](#/concept/ai-governance-programs) |
| Ethical Matrix and Explainable Fairness | Map each stakeholder's possible harms, and test whether a disparity between groups can be explained by legitimate factors. | [Algorithmic Auditing and AI Failures](#/concept/algorithmic-auditing-and-ai-failures) |

| Right Data framework | Ask six questions across problem definition, development and deployment to test whether the data behind a model is good enough. | [Enterprise Data Strategy and Governance](#/concept/enterprise-data-strategy-and-governance) |
