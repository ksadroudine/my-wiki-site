## Summary

This glossary defines the terms that appear most often in the Economy and Geopolitics part of the Book of Knowledge. Each entry gives a short plain-language definition and points to the Concept where the term is explained in depth. Terms are listed in alphabetical order.

## Glossary of Economy and Geopolitics Terms

| Term | Meaning | See |
|------|---------|-----|
| Break outs | Countries with lower digital development but the fastest momentum, such as India and Vietnam. | [Geopolitical Fragmentation and Global Expansion](#/concept/geopolitical-fragmentation-and-global-expansion) |
| Chu hai | A Chinese term meaning going overseas, used for the strategy of expanding abroad. | [Geopolitical Fragmentation and Global Expansion](#/concept/geopolitical-fragmentation-and-global-expansion) |
| Debt brake | A budget rule that limits deficits, such as Switzerland's rule of 2003, which 85 percent of voters backed. | [Rich-World Public Debt and Inflation Risk](#/concept/rich-world-public-debt-and-inflation-risk) |
| Digital Evolution Index | An index of 185 indicators across 125 countries that sorts them by digital development and momentum. | [Geopolitical Fragmentation and Global Expansion](#/concept/geopolitical-fragmentation-and-global-expansion) |
| Fiscal gap | The forward gap between future government spending and revenue. The Economist puts America's at $163 trillion. | [Rich-World Public Debt and Inflation Risk](#/concept/rich-world-public-debt-and-inflation-risk) |
| Half-life of inflation anger | The time it takes for anger about inflation to halve, which Stanford researchers estimate at about one year. | [US Affordability Debate](#/concept/us-affordability-debate) |
| Microregion | A subnational unit, such as a province, city or economic zone, that Chinese companies target instead of an entire country. | [Geopolitical Fragmentation and Global Expansion](#/concept/geopolitical-fragmentation-and-global-expansion) |
| Positional goods | Goods whose value depends on rank, such as an apartment with a view, which feel unaffordable even to high earners. | [US Affordability Debate](#/concept/us-affordability-debate) |
| Primary deficit | The budget deficit before interest payments on debt. | [Rich-World Public Debt and Inflation Risk](#/concept/rich-world-public-debt-and-inflation-risk) |
| Stall outs | Countries with high digital development but slowing momentum, such as Germany and Japan. | [Geopolitical Fragmentation and Global Expansion](#/concept/geopolitical-fragmentation-and-global-expansion) |
| Stand outs | Countries with high digital development and fast momentum, such as the United States, China and Singapore. | [Geopolitical Fragmentation and Global Expansion](#/concept/geopolitical-fragmentation-and-global-expansion) |
| Watch outs | Countries with low digital development and weak momentum, such as Pakistan and Nigeria. | [Geopolitical Fragmentation and Global Expansion](#/concept/geopolitical-fragmentation-and-global-expansion) |
