## Summary

This glossary defines the terms that appear most often in the Management and Leadership part of the Book of Knowledge. Each entry gives a short plain-language definition and points to the Concept where the term is explained in depth. Terms are listed in alphabetical order.

## Glossary of Management Terms

| Term | Meaning | See |
|------|---------|-----|
| Agency problem | The situation in which frontline negotiators are rewarded for closing any deal and not for the company's real interests. | [Enterprise Negotiation Tactics](#/concept/enterprise-negotiation-tactics) |
| BATNA | The best alternative to a negotiated agreement, which sets how much leverage a negotiator has. | [Enterprise Negotiation Tactics](#/concept/enterprise-negotiation-tactics) |
| Best-owner principle | The idea that the value of a business depends on who manages it and what strategy they pursue. | [Corporate Value Creation](#/concept/corporate-value-creation) |
| Credible commitment | A deliberate and visible reduction of one's own future options, which changes how rivals respond. | [Strategic Thinking Practice](#/concept/strategic-thinking-practice) |
| Deal value board | A board that gives negotiating teams an enterprise-wide view of related deals and brokers internal side deals. | [Enterprise Negotiation Tactics](#/concept/enterprise-negotiation-tactics) |
| Diving | A focused, high-commitment mode of learning that goes deep into one field when a strategic question demands it. | [Strategic Thinking Practice](#/concept/strategic-thinking-practice) |
| Expectations treadmill | The effect by which share prices follow changes in market expectations, so that a good company is not always a good investment. | [Corporate Value Creation](#/concept/corporate-value-creation) |
| Fungibility | The degree to which a resource can be applied to several uses. | [Organizational Bottleneck Management](#/concept/organizational-bottleneck-management) |
| Intention-behavior gap | The gap between meaning to be curious and respectful and being able to sustain it during a disagreement. | [Constructive Disagreement Language](#/concept/constructive-disagreement-language) |
| MECE | The discipline of dividing a subject into parts that are mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive. | [The Pyramid Principle](#/concept/pyramid-principle) |
| Peanut-butter problem | The spreading of attention and resources too thinly across too many priorities. | [Organizational Prioritization Frameworks](#/concept/organizational-prioritization-frameworks) |
| Planning Poker | A game in which team members reveal relative point estimates at the same time. | [Scrum Methodology](#/concept/scrum-methodology) |
| Portfolio treadmill | The need to find a replacement of similar size for every product that matures, merely to keep revenue flat. | [Corporate Value Creation](#/concept/corporate-value-creation) |
| Product Owner | The Scrum role that owns and prioritizes the backlog and is accountable for turning output into business value. | [Scrum Methodology](#/concept/scrum-methodology) |
| Resource bottleneck | A stall that arises when the resource a task needs is not available. | [Organizational Bottleneck Management](#/concept/organizational-bottleneck-management) |
| ROIC | Return on invested capital, which must exceed the cost of capital for growth to create value. | [Corporate Value Creation](#/concept/corporate-value-creation) |
| SCQA | The introduction pattern of situation, complication, question and answer. | [The Pyramid Principle](#/concept/pyramid-principle) |
| Scrum Master | The Scrum role that helps the team improve how it works and removes impediments. | [Scrum Methodology](#/concept/scrum-methodology) |
| Slack | How much of a resource is available at any time, which buffers against surges in demand. | [Organizational Bottleneck Management](#/concept/organizational-bottleneck-management) |
| Sprint | A fixed-length, non-negotiable time box, commonly two weeks, in which a Scrum team completes a set of backlog items. | [Scrum Methodology](#/concept/scrum-methodology) |
| Surfing | A broad, low-commitment mode of learning that keeps a person exposed to many fields. | [Strategic Thinking Practice](#/concept/strategic-thinking-practice) |
| Task bottleneck | A stall that arises when a task waits on the output of another task. | [Organizational Bottleneck Management](#/concept/organizational-bottleneck-management) |
| Velocity | The number of story points a team reliably completes per sprint. | [Scrum Methodology](#/concept/scrum-methodology) |
