## Summary

This reference page lists the named frameworks that appear in the Cloud part of the Book of Knowledge, in four tables. Each row says what the framework helps you do and where to read more. The frameworks are grouped by the stage of cloud work they support, from understanding the technology to realizing value and judging economics.
