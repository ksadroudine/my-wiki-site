## Summary

This reference page lists the named frameworks that appear in the Customer and Go-to-Market part of the Book of Knowledge, in four tables. Each row says what the framework helps you do and where to read more. The frameworks are grouped by topic, from understanding customers and designing products to marketing technology, generative AI and go-to-market strategy.
