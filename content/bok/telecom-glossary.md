## Summary

This glossary defines the terms that appear most often in the Telecom part of the Book of Knowledge. Each entry gives a short plain-language definition and points to the Concept where the term is explained in depth. Terms are listed in alphabetical order.

## Glossary of Telecom Terms

| Term | Meaning | See |
|------|---------|-----|
| ARPU | Average revenue per user, the revenue an operator earns from each customer. | [Telecom Pricing Power and Personalization](#/concept/telecom-pricing-and-personalization) |
| Business architecture | A blueprint of value-creation opportunities, business capabilities and technology that aligns investment with strategy. | [Telecom Structure and Organizational Transformation](#/concept/telecom-structure-and-transformation) |
| Cherry-picking | A new entrant's move of serving only the customers who pay the largest implicit tax under existing rates. | [Telecom Regulatory Economics](#/concept/telecom-regulatory-economics) |
| Complexity cost trap | The burden of maintaining legacy technology, rising labor costs and volatile outside spending that operators carry. | [Telecom Structure and Organizational Transformation](#/concept/telecom-structure-and-transformation) |
| Conglomerate discount | A lower valuation that investors apply to a company that combines unrelated businesses. | [Telecom Structure and Organizational Transformation](#/concept/telecom-structure-and-transformation) |
| Convergence | Bundling fixed, mobile and other services in a single contract. | [Telecom Pricing Power and Personalization](#/concept/telecom-pricing-and-personalization) |
| Customer graph | A unified predictive profile of each customer, built from billing, device, network and support history. | [Telecom Pricing Power and Personalization](#/concept/telecom-pricing-and-personalization) |
| Delayering | Separating an operator into a network company and one or more service companies. | [Telecom Structure and Organizational Transformation](#/concept/telecom-structure-and-transformation) |
| GPU as a service | Renting graphics processors by the hour or on reserved terms for AI training, inference or sovereign AI. | [Telecom Growth Beyond Core Connectivity](#/concept/telecom-growth-beyond-core) |
| Gold plating | Over-investment in assets that rate-of-return regulation encouraged, because a larger asset base meant a larger allowable profit. | [Telecom Regulatory Economics](#/concept/telecom-regulatory-economics) |
| Harvest now, decrypt later | The theft of encrypted data today in order to decrypt it when quantum computers can. | [Quantum Computing Enterprise Strategy](#/concept/quantum-computing-enterprise-strategy) |
| Implicit cross-subsidy | A hidden payment, such as above-cost business rates, that funds below-cost service to another group. | [Telecom Regulatory Economics](#/concept/telecom-regulatory-economics) |
| NetCo (InfraCo) | The company that operates network infrastructure and may become a carrier-neutral wholesale provider. | [Telecom Structure and Organizational Transformation](#/concept/telecom-structure-and-transformation) |
| Net neutrality | Rules that limit what content or applications a broadband provider may block or degrade. | [Telecom Regulatory Economics](#/concept/telecom-regulatory-economics) |
| Network API | A standardized interface that exposes network capabilities, such as speed on demand or location checks, to developers. | [Telecom Growth Beyond Core Connectivity](#/concept/telecom-growth-beyond-core) |
| Next-best-action | A recommendation that anticipates a customer's need, such as a retention plan, before the customer asks. | [Telecom Pricing Power and Personalization](#/concept/telecom-pricing-and-personalization) |
| Open RAN | An approach to the radio access network that uses open interfaces so that equipment from different vendors works together. | [Telecom AI Technology and Infrastructure](#/concept/telecom-ai-technology) |
| Post-quantum cryptography | Classical algorithms designed to resist attack by quantum computers. | [Quantum Computing Enterprise Strategy](#/concept/quantum-computing-enterprise-strategy) |
| Private 5G | A 5G network with a customized architecture for a specific use, such as a factory or hospital. | [Telecom Growth Beyond Core Connectivity](#/concept/telecom-growth-beyond-core) |
| Q-Day | The anticipated point at which quantum computers can break the encryption behind most digital security. | [Quantum Computing Enterprise Strategy](#/concept/quantum-computing-enterprise-strategy) |
| Quantum key distribution | A method that uses quantum properties to detect eavesdropping on an exchange of keys. | [Quantum Computing Enterprise Strategy](#/concept/quantum-computing-enterprise-strategy) |
| ServCo | A company that focuses on products, sales and customer care, separate from the network. | [Telecom Structure and Organizational Transformation](#/concept/telecom-structure-and-transformation) |
| TELRIC | The cost method for pricing leased network elements, based on what a maximally efficient carrier would spend to build the network today. | [Telecom Regulatory Economics](#/concept/telecom-regulatory-economics) |
| Unbundling | The right of entrants to lease capacity on an incumbent's network facilities at regulated rates. | [Telecom Regulatory Economics](#/concept/telecom-regulatory-economics) |
