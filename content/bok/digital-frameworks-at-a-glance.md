## Summary

This reference page lists the named frameworks that appear in the Digital Business and Platforms part of the Book of Knowledge, in four tables. Each row says what the framework helps you do and where to read more. The frameworks are grouped by topic, from transformation and APIs to platforms and the economics of information.
