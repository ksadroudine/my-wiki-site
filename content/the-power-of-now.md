## Concept

The Power of Now, by Eckhart Tolle, is a spirituality and mindfulness text arguing that most human psychological suffering originates not from present circumstances but from an unexamined identification with the mind's habitual thinking — particularly rumination on the past and anticipatory anxiety about the future — and that cultivating sustained present-moment attention loosens that identification and reduces suffering as a result. <span class="src">The Power of Now</span>
